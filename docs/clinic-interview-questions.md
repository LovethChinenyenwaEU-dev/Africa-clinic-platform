# Clinic Interview Questions (Week 1 Validation)

**Goal:** learn how clinics really work before building. Aim for 10 to 20 clinics over time; start with 3 to 5.

**Who to talk to:** clinic owner or manager, front-desk staff, a nurse, a doctor, and whoever handles billing. Talk to each role separately if you can.

**Ground rules**
- Ask about what they did *last time*, not what they *would* do. Past behaviour is more honest than opinions.
- Don't pitch. Listen. Don't mention your solution until the end.
- Never ask for or write down real patient details. Ask about the process only.
- Ask permission before taking notes, and say how you'll use them.
- Keep it to 20 to 30 minutes.

## 1. Their day (5 min)
1. Walk me through yesterday, from the moment the first patient arrives to closing.
2. What tools do you use today (paper, Excel, WhatsApp, software)?
3. How many patients do you see on a normal day, and how many staff are on duty?

## 2. Appointments and the front desk (5 min)
4. How do patients book? What happens when they don't show up?
5. How do you remind patients, and what does it cost you?
6. How do you recognise a returning patient? Have you ever had two files for one person?

## 3. Records (5 min)
7. Where are patient records kept, and what happens if that place is lost or unavailable?
8. When a patient is referred elsewhere or returns after a long gap, how do you get or share their history?
9. How do patients ask for their records, and how long does it take?

## 4. Connectivity and power (3 min)
10. What happens to your work when the internet or power goes off? How often, and for how long?
11. Do staff use their own phones for work? Which phones and which data plans?

## 5. Telemedicine (3 min)
12. Have you done consultations by phone or video? What worked and what didn't?
13. What would you never do remotely?

## 6. Money (3 min)
14. How do patients pay (cash, transfer, card, HMO)? How do you reconcile at the end of the day?
15. Where does money go missing or get disputed?

## 7. Trust and consent (2 min)
16. How do you get patient permission to share or store their information today?
17. What worries you most about putting records on a computer or in the cloud?

## 8. Wrap-up (2 min)
18. What is the single most annoying thing about running the clinic?
19. What do you pay for today that you'd happily stop paying for?
20. Who else should I talk to?

## After each interview
Record in a simple table, one row per clinic:

| Clinic | Role | Biggest pain | Tools used | Offline problem? (Y/N) | Would pay? (rough range) | Quote |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Signals to look for:** the same pain repeated by 3+ clinics, workarounds people already pay for (SMS bundles, WhatsApp groups), and a named person who would try a pilot.

**Signals to be cautious about:** polite interest with no pain story, "everyone should have this" without a specific example, or no one willing to try a pilot.
